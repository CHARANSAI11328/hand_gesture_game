# 🏍️ MOTO RUSH: 3D GESTURE GP (AI Vision Motorcycle Racing Game)

An authentic, high-speed **3D Superbike Racing Web Game** controlled **entirely with your webcam hand gestures** — powered by MediaPipe Hands and Three.js WebGL! Zero keyboard or mouse needed (with instant keyboard fallback).

---

## 🏍️ Superbike & Rider Features

1. **Fully Modeled 3D Racing Superbike**:
   - Aerodynamic Ducati Corse Red racing fairings, black carbon fiber bellypan, solo race seat, and tail cowl.
   - **Functional Wheels**: Rubber sport tires with rim spokes and perforated silver brake discs spinning at high velocity.
   - **Suspension & Chassis**: Gold anodized telescopic front forks, cast aluminum rear swingarm, exposed engine block.
   - **Xenon Headlights & Taillights**: High-power forward-casting road illumination spotlight and intense red taillight LEDs.
   - **Twin Titanium Exhausts with Nitro Flames**: Blue/cyan fire cones that ignite and stretch during nitro boost.
2. **Articulated 3D Biker (Rider Model)**:
   - Full racing leather suit with colored sponsor stripes and shoulder protection.
   - Aerodynamic racing helmet with glossy mirror visor.
   - Biker hands holding clip-on handlebars and feet tucked on rear-set footpegs.
   - **MotoGP Knee-Down Action**: When leaning into a corner, the rider shifts weight and drops their inside knee slider towards the tarmac!
   - **Power Wheelie on Boost**: When hitting nitro boost, the front wheel lifts off the asphalt into a power wheelie!
   - **Brake Suspension Dive**: When braking, front forks compress and the rider sits up slightly into the wind.
3. **100% Glitch-Free Seamless Highway**:
   - Single continuous highway mesh with dynamic UV texture scrolling — **zero teleportation seams, zero gaps, zero flickering**.
   - Crisp white dashed lane stripes, outer shoulder lines, and red-white racing rumble strip kerbs.
   - Overhead streetlights, safety guardrails, and distant cyber skyscraper skyline.
4. **Traffic Dodging & Close Passes (Near-Miss System)**:
   - Civilian traffic cars (taxis, sedans, SUVs) cruising along highway lanes.
   - **Near-Miss Bonus**: Skimming past traffic at high speed triggers instant combo multipliers (`+250 x combo`) and swoosh audio!
   - Collectible Gold Trophy Coins, Nitro Fuel Canisters, and Electromagnetic Force Shields.

## 🎮 Hand Gesture Motorcycle Controls

| Hand Gesture | Racing Action | What It Does |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Move Hand Left** 🖐️ | ⇦ **Lean Left** | Banks the superbike into the left lane and drops left knee slider |
| **Keep Hand Centered** 🖐️ | ◉ **Upright Center** | Tucks forward in an aerodynamic straight-line speed run |
| **Move Hand Right** 🖐️ | ⇨ **Lean Right** | Banks the superbike into the right lane and drops right knee slider |
| **Raise Hand Up / Fist** ☝️✊ | 🚀 **Nitro Throttle** | Pops a power wheelie with twin blue exhaust flames for 4.5s of invulnerable speed |
| **Lower Hand Down** 👇 | 🛑 **Rear Brake** | Lights up brake LEDs, compresses front forks, and drops speed safely |

*Instant Keyboard Fallbacks: `A`/`D` or `Left`/`Right` (Steer & Lean), `W` or `Shift` (Nitro Wheelie), `S` or `Down` (Rear Brake), `P`/`Esc` (Pause).*

## 🚀 How to Launch and Play

1. Open in your browser: **[http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)**
2. Click **Allow** on the browser webcam permission prompt.
3. Test your hand movements on the start screen.
4. Click **🏍️ START RACE** and carve through highway traffic!
